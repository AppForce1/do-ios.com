Stability in tech is disappearing. Modern engineers are no longer expected to master one stack for years—they’re expected to reinvent themselves repeatedly.

This talk explores the psychological impact of constant technological change: what happens when your expertise suddenly feels outdated, your confidence drops, and you’re expected to perform while feeling like a beginner again.

More importantly, we’ll explore how to adapt without losing yourself. Rather than resisting change or chasing every new trend, attendees will learn practical ways to build a healthier relationship with uncertainty, approach career transitions with confidence, and stay resilient even when their expertise no longer feels enough.

Instead of asking “How do I keep up?”, we’ll ask a different question: “How do I stay confident when I can’t keep up with everything?”

## Ekaterina Volkova

Ekaterina Volkova is a Software Engineer at Zalando, where she builds products used by millions of customers. She has spoken at international conferences including Swift Heroes, NS Spain, and SwiftLeeds. Her talks combine real engineering experience with the human side of software development, exploring how developers grow, adapt and thrive in an industry that never stops changing.
