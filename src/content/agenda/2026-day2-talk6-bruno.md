Stand out from other apps with your own great set of symbols! Custom SF Symbols are a great way to make your own symbols with the same flexibility as Apple’s own. They’re also a pain to create and to get right, which is why we often just make our square icons and call it a day. So let’s change that! You’ll learn how you can use SF Symbols, why you’d want custom ones, and how to get started on creating them. From mass producing symbols out of existing icons, to creating hand-crafted pieces of art in every weight!

## Bruno Scheele

Bruno Scheele, of Binary Adventures, has been developing apps since 2010 and has worked for many different clients, from small to big. His interest in making software that can be used by anyone and everyone leads him to consider inclusivity in all aspects of development. Accessibility especially has captured his imagination, and he now strives to keep learning more about this whole new world and help companies and organizations make truly usable apps for everyone.
