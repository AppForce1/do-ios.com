A live demo that starts where typical penetration testing ends. In this session, I will attempt to break into a “secure” iOS application on a jailbroken device, targeting common protections such as runtime checks and secure communication mechanisms.

Participants will learn how these defenses behave under real-world conditions, where they fail, and what that means for application design. The session highlights practical attack surfaces in iOS apps and translates them into actionable insights developers can use to build more resilient systems.

Will I succeed? That’s part of the journey.

## Cyril Cermak

Hi I am Cyril, an iOS System Architect with over a decade of experience building cutting-edge mobile applications. I am specializing in leading teams to deliver state-of-the-art mobile engineering solutions. My professional passions include software engineering, iOS security, modular architecture, and reverse engineering iOS apps. I frequently share my expertise through workshops and talks, aiming to inspire others and drive innovation and a practical security mindset within the mobile development community.
