As teams grow and features multiply, many apps fall into a trap of inconsistency and technical debt. This session tackles this challenge head-on, moving beyond the basics to demonstrate how a well-architected design system is the key to maintaining velocity, consistency and quality in any large-scale SwiftUI project.

This talk provides a practical blueprint for building a scalable SwiftUI application by architecting a robust design system with reusable, accessible and testable components.

Join this session to gain practical strategies for scaling your apps, ensuring UI consistency and delivering a high-quality, accessible user experience.

## Raluca Bujor

Raluca is an iOS Chapter Lead at Deutsche Bank with 8 years of experience in iOS development. She is passionate about SwiftUI, modern mobile architecture, and building scalable, user-friendly applications.

Beyond engineering, she enjoys organizing iOS community meetups, mentoring developers, and sharing knowledge with fellow mobile engineers. Outside of tech, she loves fitness, staying active, and bringing the same consistency and discipline from sports into everyday work.
