Foundation Models is how your app connects to Apple Intelligence. In this hands-on, fast moving workshop you’ll learn the fundamentals of using Foundation Models on device with sessions, prompts, instructions, and tools. We’ll then move on to this year’s additions to Foundation Models including private cloud compute, images, dynamic profiles, and the evaluations framework.

**Required tools:** A Mac running Xcode 27 and either a device you can deploy to using iOS 27 or macOS 27.

## Daniel Steinberg

Daniel loves to write about, teach and code for Apple platforms using Swift. Daniel is the author of more than a dozen books including the best-selling books A Functional Programming Kickstart, A SwiftUI Kickstart, A Swift Kickstart and Dear Elena. Daniel presents iOS, Functional Programming, SwiftUI, and Swift training and consults through his company Dim Sum Thinking.
