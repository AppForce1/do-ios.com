Developers and designers have always faced the challenge of making apps feel native on different devices without maintaining multiple codebases. With the iPad, Vision Pro, and now the foldable iPhone Duo, the hardware lineup is more complex than ever. The natural instinct is to design platform-by-platform: iPhone first, then iPad, then Mac. But Apple’s design philosophy is the exact opposite: design for the axis, not the device.

This session covers a universal approach to building adaptive apps through three core areas: navigation, interactivity, and platform features. We will look at how a single, well-structured foundation can change shape from a compact 6-inch screen to a multi-display fold, all the way to a 3D window.

## Emanuele Agosta

Emanuele is a product designer and Apple Certified Trainer. He was a senior design mentor at the Apple Developer Academy in Naples from 2019 to 2025, designing curriculum and mentoring 300+ international developers a year in UX/UI, product design, spatial computing, and accessibility. He now works on UX and learning design at Tesla in Amsterdam.
