visionOS is often framed around entertainment, but some of its most compelling uses are in business. This talk explores where spatial computing is already creating practical value in areas like retail, real estate, healthcare, training, and industrial workflows. I’ll look at the patterns shared by successful business-oriented visionOS apps, where spatial interaction genuinely improves the experience, and where it just adds novelty. The session is intended to help developers think more clearly about product fit, UX, and opportunity: how to evaluate whether a visionOS idea solves a real problem, and what kinds of business scenarios are most promising today.

## Matvei Novoselov

I am an iOS and visionOS Software Engineer working in London, UK at Sony Group. Apple Developer Academy Alumni. Apple Swift Student Challenge 2025 winner. Co-author of a scientific research paper on visionOS and Spatial Computing at the ICSR International Conference on Social Robotics published by Springer. Co-author of Virtual Film Maker Patent SYP361478US01.
