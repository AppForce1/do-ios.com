In this hands-on workshop, you will learn how to use Apple's Foundation Models framework, Gemini, and Firebase AI Logic to build an AI-powered meal planning app.

We will cover the following topics:

- How to use Apple's Foundation Models framework for local, on-device AI
- Connecting Apple Foundation Models to Gemini via Firebase AI Logic
- Generating structured output
- Analysing images
- Generating images from text prompts
- Managing prompts (and protecting your intellectual property)
- Chat and streaming output
- Bi-directional audio and video streaming for captivating user experiences
- Protecting your app using App Check

This is a hands-on tutorial using DocC, so people will be able to work at their own pace. I will explain all relevant concepts in detail using slides and live demos.

**Note:** To join the workshop, you must **bring your own laptop with the latest version of Xcode installed, as well as a physical iPhone to deploy your app to!** The activities are intended for Mac laptops and cannot be completed on a tablet or phone.

### Who should attend?

This hands-on workshop is designed for iOS developers who want to add powerful AI capabilities to their applications without compromising security or performance. If you are comfortable with SwiftUI and looking to move beyond basic API wrappers into production-grade AI features, this workshop is for you.

## Peter Friese

Peter is a Staff Developer Advocate on the Firebase team at Google, helping developers build amazing experiences and high quality apps using Firebase and AI.

With a passion for empowering developers and fostering innovation, Peter works tirelessly with the Firebase team to make his vision of “cutting short the time to magic” a reality.

Peter is also the author of the book Asynchronous Programming with SwiftUI and Combine: Functional Programming to Build UIs on Apple Platforms and host of the YouTube show Firebase After Hours, in which he and his colleagues explore Firebase in a light-hearted and fun setting.
