If you work on an iOS medical app that will go through FDA approval process soon, you probably would like to know what it feels like? You can go through this adventure with this presentation!

Preparing the code and documentation, highlighting certain functional and security features — all these topics will be covered. You will find out what is the most important in your codebase in the eyes of the regulatory, why do you need even more resources for the documentation than for the debugging and get an insight on how to generate SBOMs for the projects without Package.swift.

Experienced iOS developer based in The Netherlands, worked in different fields and ended in healthcare startup. Every Apple event drinks apple cider and eats apple cakes. Easily distracted by cows.
